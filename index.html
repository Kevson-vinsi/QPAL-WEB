Our history

Before QPAL became a clan, it began with just two individuals who met while playing ranked matches in Dueling Grounds. Through their shared interest in the game, they eventually created a Discord server and established a private community specifically for Filipinos who played Dueling Grounds.

As the community continued to grow, new friendships were formed and valuable connections were established. With the increasing number of members and the growing sense of community, the founders decided to expand its purpose beyond being a private community. QPAL would become both a clan and an open community, welcoming not only players from the Philippines but also international players who shared the same passion and desire to connect and grow together.

On July 13, 2026, the QPAL Clan was officially established. From its humble beginnings as a small community of two individuals, QPAL has grown with the vision of becoming a community-driven clan built upon friendship, unity, and personal growth.

QPAL stands, and will continue to stand, as more than just a gaming clan. It is a community where players can build meaningful friendships, create lasting connections, support one another, and grow together both within and beyond the game.
